# Inkwell privacy policy

Inkwell is a personal notes app. It has no servers of its own and collects no analytics.

- Your notes, tasks and attachments are encrypted on your device with your passphrase before they are synced.
- If you choose Google Drive sync, the encrypted data is stored only in the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive. Inkwell can't see any other file in your Drive.
- If you choose GitHub sync, the encrypted data is stored in your own private GitHub repository.
- Your Google email address is read once to show which account is signed in. It stays on your device.
- Voice input uses your device's own speech service. Inkwell does not record or upload audio.
- Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party. You can delete your data at any time by disconnecting in Settings and removing the Drive app data or the GitHub repository.
